# Third party

`dist/index.html` embeds the Latin glyphs of Instrument Serif (regular and italic)
as web fonts for the page headings. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License
1.1, copied in [`licenses/INSTRUMENT-SERIF-OFL.txt`](licenses/INSTRUMENT-SERIF-OFL.txt).

The picture itself uses only fonts already on your device. Nothing else is bundled
at runtime. Development uses Playwright and esbuild.
